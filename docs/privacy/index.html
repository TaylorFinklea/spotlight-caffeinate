Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 25, 2026

Spotlight Caffeinate is designed to work locally on your Mac. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

Spotlight Caffeinate does not collect personal data from you and does not send your app usage data to a remote server.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your Mac so the app can function:

  • saved presets
  • recent session history
  • automation rules and automation run history
  • local app preferences such as menu bar display options

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Spotlight Caffeinate uses local macOS notifications to alert you when a session finishes. These notifications are generated on-device and are not sent through a third-party service operated by us.

Calendar Access

If you choose to use calendar-based automations, the app may request access to your calendars. Calendar data is used only on-device to evaluate optional automation rules. We do not collect, transmit, or store your calendar data on remote servers.

Analytics, Advertising, And Accounts

Spotlight Caffeinate does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, user accounts, or remote profile tracking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information because Spotlight Caffeinate does not collect personal information from the app in the first place.

Changes To This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about Spotlight Caffeinate, contact support@finklea.dev.
